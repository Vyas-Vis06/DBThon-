# Rule 4 gear catalogue seed

This catalogue transcribes the 44 labels listed in Rule 4(i)–(xliv) of the official 2013 Rules PDF. The primary text was read from PDF pages 3–4 (Gazette pages 20–21) using the bundled PDF reader. Labels, capitalization, and apparent odd spellings are preserved as printed; apparent duplicates or ambiguities are not silently corrected. `PRIMARY_TEXT_VERIFIED` refers only to the transcription against that supplied primary text.

This is a catalogue of listed items, not a determination that every item must be issued to every worker or used on every job. The Rules’ Rule 2(1)(g) describes applicability with reference to the specific nature of the work. Accordingly, `requirement_scope` is `UNASSIGNED` and `default_required` is `false` for every row pending a separately reviewed operational mapping. Those values do not negate the catalogue entry or state a legal conclusion.

No PPE requirements, work-specific selections, or legal interpretations are made here. The catalogue does not itself grant legal authorization to conduct work or entry. Do not automatically require all 44 items for each worker. Any operational selection requires a separate, explicit decision and appropriate review.
